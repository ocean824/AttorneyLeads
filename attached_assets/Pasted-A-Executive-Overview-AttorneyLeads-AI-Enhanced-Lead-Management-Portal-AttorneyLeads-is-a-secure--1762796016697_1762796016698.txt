A) Executive Overview

AttorneyLeads: AI-Enhanced Lead Management Portal

AttorneyLeads is a secure, admin-only React + Node.js platform for managing attorney leads, equipped with 2FA (TOTP), trusted device recognition, and modular AI enhancements. It’s designed for deployment on Replit, supporting PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM and session-based authentication.
